Frequently asked
Who can use PSCA HRMS?
PSCA HRMS is for employees of the Punjab Safe Cities Authority. There is no public sign-up: accounts are created by HR, and you sign in with your employee ID and the password HR gives you.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Passwords are reset by HR. Please contact HR to have your password reset, then sign in with your employee ID and the new password.
I can't sign in on my new phone.
Your account is linked to your registered device. The sign-in screen shows your phone's Device ID, which you can copy with a tap. Send it, along with your employee ID, to info@psca.gop.pk so your new device can be registered.
I'm not receiving notifications.
Make sure notifications are allowed for PSCA HRMS in your phone's settings. On iPhone, go to Settings → Notifications → PSCA HRMS; on Android, go to Settings → Apps → PSCA HRMS → Notifications. Then sign out and sign in again.
How do I report a technical issue with the app?
Email info@psca.gop.pk with your employee ID, your phone model, a description of the issue, and a screenshot if you have one.
How do I request a new feature?
Send your suggestion to info@psca.gop.pk. We read every message from our users.
Who builds and maintains this app?
PSCA HRMS is developed and maintained by the Punjab Safe Cities Authority.
